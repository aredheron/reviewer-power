CLASSIFY INFORMATIVE NAMES
First strip leading and trailing underscores; these indicate private/dummy names,
not multiword naming style. For the remaining name:
- Neutral: empty, or a lowercase single segment with optional digits, such as x,
  result, item2, _cache, or value_. Neutral names do not supply a target form.
- snake_case: lower-case/digit segments separated by one or more single internal
  underscores, e.g. item_count, converted_values, _item_count, item_2.
  Every segment must be nonempty and contain only lowercase letters/digits;
  the first character must be a lowercase letter.
- camelCase: starts lowercase and contains at least one uppercase letter, with
  no internal underscores; subsequent characters are letters/digits, e.g.
  itemCount, convertedValues, parseHTTP, item2Count.
- other: any remaining informative local/helper name, including PascalCase,
  ALL_CAPS, mixedCase_with_underscore, doubled__internal_underscores, or non-ASCII
  names outside these definitions. Do not guess word boundaries for lowercase
  concatenations like itemcount; those are neutral.
